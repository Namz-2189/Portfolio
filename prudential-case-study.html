01 — CONTEXT

Demand for digital is growing ↗

Indonesia's insurance penetration sits at just 3% — one of the lowest in Southeast Asia — despite a population of 270 million, over half of whom are under 35. Prudential and FWD Mega Life partnered to capture this gap with Way, a fully digital insurance platform. The brief was clear: make insurance feel accessible, trustworthy, and fast.

3% Insurance penetration in Indonesia — among the lowest in SEA
270M Population, with 50%+ digital-native millennials & Gen Z
68% Of users abandoned the previous flow before completing a purchase
📱
Mobile-first market
90%+ of internet usage in Indonesia happens on mobile devices. A desktop-first insurance experience simply doesn't reach this audience.
🔒
Trust is the barrier
Qualitative research revealed that 71% of non-insured users cited distrust and complexity as the primary reasons for not purchasing insurance.
⚡
Speed matters
Digital-first users expect to complete a purchase in minutes, not days. Legacy insurance flows with paper forms and agent callbacks create fatal drop-offs.

02 — RESEARCH

Understanding the Problem

Before designing anything, we spent 3 weeks deeply understanding users — who they are, what they fear, and why they give up. We ran 18 moderated user interviews, 2 diary studies, and a heuristic audit of 6 competing apps.

"I tried to buy insurance online once. I gave up after 20 minutes — too many questions, too many confusing terms. I didn't trust it."

— User interview participant, 28 · Jakarta

"If the app looked modern and the price was upfront, I'd probably just buy it right there. No need for an agent."

— User interview participant, 31 · Surabaya
Key finding 01
Users couldn't understand what they were buying. Product descriptions were full of jargon with no plain-language explanation of coverage or exclusions.
Key finding 02
Premium calculation was a black box. Users wanted to see how their choices affect price in real time — not receive a quote by email 24 hours later.
Key finding 03
Claim submission was deeply intimidating. Users feared they'd do it wrong and lose their claim — a perception that deterred purchases entirely.

Pattern 01 — Transparency
The strongest apps displayed price, coverage, and exclusions upfront — no surprises, no small print hidden behind menus.
✦
Pattern 02 — Guided flows
Best-in-class onboarding used conversational, step-by-step flows rather than long-form data entry. Users felt assisted, not interrogated.
✦
Pattern 03 — Instant feedback
Real-time premium calculation as users adjust inputs was the single feature most correlated with purchase completion across every competitor.

04 — DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Defining the solution

Research synthesis gave us four guiding principles that shaped every design decision — from information architecture to micro-interactions.

01
Radical transparency
Show the full price, what's covered, and what's excluded — before the user asks. No hidden terms, no jargon. If a concept needs a definition, embed it inline.
02
Progressive disclosure
Only ask for what's needed right now. Break complex forms into conversational, single-question steps. Show complexity only when the user is ready for it.
03
Real-time confidence
Every user input that affects price or coverage should update instantly. The user should always know where they stand — price, status, protection level.
04
Guided, not gated
Help users make good decisions with smart defaults, contextual tooltips, and AI-powered suggestions. Assistance should feel like a knowledgeable friend, not a chatbot.

05 — KEY FEATURES

Bringing the solution to life

Five core design decisions moved the needle from a frustrating experience to one users described as "finally, an app that just makes sense."

FEATURE 01

Live premium calculation

Replaced the old quote-by-email model with a real-time premium slider. As users adjust age, coverage amount, and term, the monthly price updates instantly — no waiting, no uncertainty.

Slider inputs with debounced API calls keep the UI responsive
Price is always visible in a sticky footer during configuration
Annual vs monthly toggle with instant recalculation
FEATURE 02

AI assistance & plain language

Every insurance term, exclusion, and benefit now has an inline explainer. Users can tap any highlighted word to get a plain-language definition. An AI assistant answers questions conversationally without leaving the flow.

Contextual definitions replace policy document links
AI chat available throughout — no need to call an agent
Smart suggestions nudge users toward appropriate coverage tiers
FEATURE 03

Redesigned product cards

The old product listing was a wall of text. The new card design leads with a single, clear benefit statement, shows the starting price, and uses visual coverage indicators so users can compare at a glance.

Card hierarchy: headline benefit → starting price → key inclusions
Visual "what's covered" bar replaces bullet list
Social proof (X users this month) adds trust without clutter
FEATURE 04

USP education module

A short, swipeable onboarding module educates users on what makes Way different — before asking them to buy anything. Trust is built before the transaction begins.

3-screen swipeable explainer at first launch
Animated benefit illustrations keep attention
Skippable — respects users who already know what they want
FEATURE 05

Streamlined policy & claims

Post-purchase is where trust is won or lost. We redesigned the policy dashboard and claims submission into a guided, step-by-step flow that takes under 3 minutes to complete.

Policy status visible at a glance — no digging required
Claims checklist tells users exactly what documents are needed
Real-time claim status updates with push notifications
✦
