01 — CONTEXT

Demand for digital is growing

Insurance is often viewed as a complex and confusing field, leaving users uncertain about policies, claims, and benefits. With millennials and Gen-Z shaping digital trends, there is a growing demand for fast, simple, and seamless service experiences. The existing Way app struggled with user-generating traffic, sales, and driving conversions.

02 — RESEARCH

Understanding the Problem

To make our app user-centred we began our process by deep diving into understanding user behaviours, pain points, and expectations. We conducted design audits of the existing app, conducted user and stakeholder interviews, and ran Competitor Analysis.

⚠️

The app lacked a focus on building trust, despite Prudential's credibility as a joint venture.

🎨

Inconsistent design and lack of visual appeal to encourage clicks and drive user engagement.

🗺️

Difficulty in navigating the key benefits of different insurance options available on the platform.

🔒

For returning customers, the app was inconvenient, displaying the login prompt only when selecting "Buy Now".

03 — COMPETITOR ANALYSIS

Learning from the best

To get better insights we looked into competitor brands and anchor Indonesia to better understand what was working well and what our competitors are doing better in this process. We analysed Lemonade, geico, and Prudential peers.

04 — DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Defining the solution

Our research-driven approach led us to focus on key design principles that enhanced usability and trust across the entire Way experience.

🧭
Simplified Navigation
Implemented a bottom nav bar with clear labels — home, explore, claims, and an easy-to-access support — so users always know where they are.
📱
Mobile-First Design
By focusing on mobile traffic we designed key touch-friendly gestures, hidden mobile menu, and responsive layouts for seamless accessibility.
👁️
Clear Visual Hierarchy
The design components create a clear order of importance, visually directing users to the most relevant and actionable content to improve accessibility.
🏆
Brand-Driven Trust
Integrated Prudential brand elements to help users feel confident and familiar with the platform, connecting to a trusted brand name and experience.

05 — KEY FEATURES

Bringing the solution to life

From intelligent premium calculators to AI-assisted guidance, each feature was designed to reduce friction and build user confidence.

CORE

Premium calculation

Help users understand their insurance cost and coverage options based on personalised inputs — age, plan type, and frequency of payment — at a glance.

AI

AI for assistance

An intelligent chatbot delivers fast, intuitive, and user-friendly guidance — helping users navigate policies, schedule calls, and complete purchases conversationally.

PRODUCT

Product card

Product cards highlighting the key benefit and clearest premium amount for easy decision making, with one-tap access to full policy details.

USP

Product USP

Highlighting the primary product USP in the onboarding steps allows users to understand the main benefit of the product clearly during the flow.

FLOW

Premium Steps & Policy card

A guided step-by-step premium flow paired with a clear policy summary card — letting users review benefits, cover details, and cost before commitment.
